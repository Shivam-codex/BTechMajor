1. INTRODUCTION
The Drainage Department oversees the maintenance of underground sewage lines, storm-water drains, open nullahs, culverts, and manhole safety infrastructure to prevent public health hazards and monsoon waterlogging.

2. TYPES OF DRAINAGE COMPLAINTS
- Open, broken, or dislodged manhole covers (Severe Public Safety Hazard).
- Underground sewage pipeline chokes causing wastewater backflow into homes or cellars.
- Overflowing stormwater drains spilling foul water onto public roads.
- Blocked natural drainage nullahs leading to localized neighborhood flooding.
- Excessive mosquito breeding due to stagnant wastewater pools.

3. CITIZEN ACTION STEPS
Step 1: In case of an open or missing manhole cover, immediately raise a complaint marked Critical.
Step 2: Provide landmark details (e.g., in front of shop X, near junction Y, Ward Z).
Step 3: Suction-cum-jetting sewer cleaning machines are deployed by the regional drainage unit.
Step 4: Real-time resolution notes and verification are recorded upon completion.

4. RESOLUTION TIMELINES (SLA)
- Missing or broken manhole cover: Within 2 to 4 hours (Immediate Barricading & Replacement).
- Sewage water backflow into residential premises: Within 12 hours.
- General drain choke or gutter silt removal: 24 to 48 hours.
- Nullah de-silting and structural wall repairs: 5 to 7 working days.

5. HEALTH PROTOCOL
Upon unblocking sanitary sewer lines, municipal sanitation workers are mandated to spray disinfectants and lime powder around the area to neutralize odors and disease vectors.
