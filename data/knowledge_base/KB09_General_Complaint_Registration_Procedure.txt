1. INTRODUCTION TO CITIZEN GRIEVANCE PORTAL
The Smart City Complaint Management System provides a unified, transparent digital window for citizens to file grievances related to any municipal civic amenity without needing to visit physical municipal corporation offices.

2. SUPPORTED SUBMISSION CHANNELS
- Direct Online Text Submission: Enter grievance details directly into the web portal.
- Document Upload: Upload pre-drafted grievance letters, scanned notices, or citizen petitions in PDF, DOCX, or TXT formats (up to 10 MB).
- Bilingual Grievance Input: The system natively supports English, Marathi (Devanagari script), and code-mixed inputs.

3. LIFECYCLE OF A COMPLAINT
1. Submission: The citizen submits the grievance and is assigned an immutable tracking ID (CMP-YYYY-XXXXXX).
2. NLP & Rule Classification: The system pre-processes the grievance and assigns category, priority, and department deterministically.
3. Department Assignment: Automatically routed to the jurisdictional department engineer or officer.
4. Investigation & Action (In Progress): Field staff investigate and execute restorative work.
5. Resolution: The officer logs resolution notes and marks the grievance as 'Resolved'.
6. Quality Review: Unclassified or complex grievances are placed under 'Needs Review' for administrative intervention.

4. TRACKING YOUR COMPLAINT
Citizens can track real-time grievance status 24/7 by entering their Complaint ID on the portal dashboard.
