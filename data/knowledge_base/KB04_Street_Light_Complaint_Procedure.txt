1. POLICY OVERVIEW
Proper public street lighting is essential for pedestrian safety, women safety at night, and vehicular accident prevention. The Electrical Department manages all municipal LED streetlights, sodium vapor lamps, high-mast towers, and feeder pillars.

2. CLASSIFICATION OF STREET LIGHT FAULTS
- Dark streets with single or multiple non-functional street lamps.
- Continuous day-and-night burning lights indicating timer switch or photocell sensor failure.
- Tilting, rusted, or structurally damaged light poles liable to fall.
- Flickering fixtures or low lumen illumination in sensitive residential areas.
- Exposed junction boxes or loose wiring at the base of street lamp posts.

3. REPORTING PROCEDURE
Step 1: Check the alphanumeric Pole Number stenciled on the lamp pole (e.g., SL-W04-0128).
Step 2: Submit a complaint via the Citizen Portal with the pole number and street address.
Step 3: The Electrical Department maintenance van is dispatched based on ward jurisdiction.
Step 4: Once repaired, the technician submits photographic verification to close the grievance.

4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLA)
- Individual street lamp replacement (LED fixture/choke failure): 24 hours.
- Circuit failure / entire street blackout (feeder pillar fault): 12 hours.
- Damaged pole or exposed live wire at pole base: 4 hours (Emergency Priority).
- High-mast tower repairs: 48 to 72 hours.

5. SPECIAL NIGHT PATROLLING
Municipal Electrical Squads conduct weekly night surveys across arterial roads to proactively identify dark patches.
