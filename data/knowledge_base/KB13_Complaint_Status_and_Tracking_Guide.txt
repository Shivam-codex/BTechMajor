1. MEANING OF COMPLAINT STATUSES
Every municipal grievance progresses through defined lifecycle stages:

- 'Submitted': The grievance has been successfully recorded in the database and assigned a unique tracking ID.
- 'Classified': Deterministic NLP rules have analyzed the grievance, assigned category keywords, and computed the Rule Match Score.
- 'Assigned': The grievance has been routed to the appropriate department (e.g., Water Supply, Sanitation, Roads) and ward office.
- 'In Progress': The assigned field engineer or sanitation supervisor has initiated inspection or restorative works.
- 'Resolved': Remedial actions have been completed, verified on-site, and logged with resolution notes.
- 'Needs Review': Grievances that did not match predefined rule thresholds or require manual administrative verification.
- 'Rejected': Duplicate submissions, frivolous grievances, or requests outside municipal jurisdiction.

2. TRACKING INSTRUCTIONS
1. Visit the portal and navigate to the 'Track Complaint' tab.
2. Enter your Complaint ID (e.g., CMP-2026-00123).
3. The tracking interface displays category, priority, department, timestamps, and full rule matching transparency.
