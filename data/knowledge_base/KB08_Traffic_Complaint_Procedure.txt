1. OVERVIEW
The Traffic Management Department, in close coordination with the City Traffic Police, handles citizen grievances concerning vehicular congestion bottlenecks, non-functional traffic signals, illegal roadside parking, unauthorized hawker encroachments on roads, and safety crossings.

2. COMPLAINT TYPES
- Non-functional traffic signals or faulty cycle timing causing severe traffic gridlock.
- Illegal parking of commercial vehicles, trucks, or abandoned scrap cars blocking traffic lanes.
- Encroachments by roadside stalls or construction debris causing traffic lane bottlenecks.
- Missing zebra crossings, stop lines, or missing directional signage at major crossroads.
- Unregulated wrong-side vehicular movement creating severe accident hazards.

3. REPORTING MECHANISM
Step 1: State the exact intersection name, road stretch, and landmark.
Step 2: Upload a brief note or picture showing the congestion or malfunctioning signal.
Step 3: Traffic control division directs technical signal repair teams and field wardens.

4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLA)
- Major traffic signal power failure or amber blinking fault: Within 2 hours.
- Removal of broken-down vehicle or towing of illegal parking bottleneck: Within 3 hours.
- Zebra crossing repainting and traffic signage installation: 7 working days.
- Comprehensive traffic regulation survey for bottleneck spots: 14 working days.

5. SPECIAL HELPLINE
Traffic emergencies and acute junction blockages can be reported to the Traffic Control Room at 1095 / 020-2612-XXXX.
