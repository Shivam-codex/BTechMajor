1. SCOPE AND STANDARDS
The Public Health and Sanitation Department ensures that community toilets, public urinals, and Sulabh Shauchalaya complexes maintain clean, hygienic, water-connected, and well-ventilated public sanitation facilities for all citizens, including disabled-accessible units.

2. NATURE OF GRIEVANCES
- Unhygienic conditions, dirty toilet pans, lack of regular deep cleaning or sweeping.
- Absence of running water supply, broken taps, or defective flush systems.
- Broken doors, missing latch/lock mechanisms, or absent electric lights inside cubicles.
- Overflowing septic tanks or septic gas odors in toilet surroundings.
- Overcharging or refusal of access by toilet maintenance contractors.

3. SUBMITTING A GRIEVANCE
Citizens can lodge a grievance by identifying the toilet block code (displayed on the facade of every public restroom facility) or by stating the landmark (e.g., Near City Bus Stand, Market Yard).

4. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLA)
- Emergency cleaning of heavily soiled toilet blocks: Within 4 hours.
- Restoration of interrupted water supply to public restrooms: 6 to 12 hours.
- Minor plumbing, door latch, and electric light repairs: 24 to 48 hours.
- Septic tank emptying by suction tanker: 24 hours.

5. HYGIENE AUDIT
Every public toilet undergoes bi-weekly quality audits by municipal Health Inspectors to certify cleanliness ratings.
