1. OBJECTIVE OF THE ESCALATION HIERARCHY
To ensure accountability, prevent bureaucratic delays, and ensure strict compliance with Citizen Charter SLAs, unresolved complaints are automatically escalated through three supervisory administrative levels.

2. THE THREE-TIER ESCALATION MATRIX
- Level 1 (Initial Assignment - Day 0 to SLA Expiry):
  Designated Officer: Junior Engineer (JE) / Sanitary Inspector / Field Supervisor.
  Responsibility: Site inspection, work order issuance, and physical fault resolution within standard SLA.

- Level 2 (First Escalation - SLA + 24 to 48 Hours Unresolved):
  Designated Officer: Deputy Executive Engineer (DEE) / Ward Medical Officer of Health.
  Action: Review reason for delay, reallocate resources or contractor notices, direct intervention.

- Level 3 (Final Escalation - SLA + 72 Hours Unresolved):
  Designated Officer: Executive Engineer (EE) / Additional Municipal Commissioner (AMC).
  Action: Disciplinary inquiry, departmental penalty on contractor/engineer, priority resolution mandate.

3. CITIZEN-INITIATED RE-OPENING
If a complaint is marked 'Resolved' but the physical fault persists on the ground, citizens can re-open the complaint within 7 days by submitting resolution feedback. Re-opened complaints automatically escalate to Level 2.
