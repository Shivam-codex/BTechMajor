Q1: How do I report a pothole on my street?
A1: You can submit a complaint via the Citizen Portal with your street name and nearest landmark. You can also upload a photo or document. The system will categorize it under 'Road/Pothole' and route it to the Roads and Infrastructure Department.

Q2: What is the normal turnaround time for pothole repairs?
A2: Potholes on major arterial corridors are attended to within 24 to 48 hours using quick-setting cold mix. Potholes on internal colony roads are scheduled within 3 to 5 working days.

Q3: Can I file a complaint in Marathi or mixed Marathi-English?
A3: Yes! The system natively processes English, Marathi in Devanagari script (e.g., 'रस्त्यावर खड्डे आहेत', 'पाणी येत नाही'), and code-mixed inputs without requiring manual translation.

Q4: What should I do if there is an exposed live wire or severe electric sparking?
A4: An exposed electrical wire is a Critical life-threatening hazard. Immediately stay at a safe distance and call the Emergency Helpline at 1800-233-0001 or submit an emergency complaint. The Electrical Department responds within 1 to 4 hours.

Q5: How can I check the status of my submitted complaint?
A5: You can use your unique Complaint ID (e.g., CMP-2026-AB12CD) on the portal tracking page to view the current status, assigned engineer, and resolution updates.

Q6: How long does garbage collection resolution take if bins are overflowing?
A6: Overflowing community garbage bins and missed garbage truck pickups are scheduled for clearance within 8 to 24 hours by the Sanitation Department.
