--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Low Water Pressure / Supply Failure          24 to 48 Hours

Overflowing Community Garbage Bin            8 to 12 Hours
Missed Garbage Vehicle Routine               24 Hours
Pothole Repair on Arterial Roads             24 to 48 Hours

2. ACCOUNTABILITY RULES
Failure to resolve grievances within the designated SLA mandates an automatic system trigger elevating the complaint to higher supervisory authorities without manual citizen prompting.
