1. OVERVIEW AND OBJECTIVE
This document details citizen guidelines and complaint processes concerning daily municipal door-to-door waste collection, street sweeping, community dustbin clearance, and eradication of illegal open garbage dumps.

2. TYPES OF SANITATION GRIEVANCES
- Ghanta Gadi (municipal garbage collection vehicle) not arriving on schedule.
- Overflowing public community dustbins in residential societies and market centers.
- Open littering, illegal dumping of commercial or construction debris on roadsides.
- Dead animal removal and biological waste hazard clearance.
- Failure of sanitary staff to sweep public roads and lanes.

3. CITIZEN SUBMISSION PROCESS
Step 1: Citizens can submit a complaint via the Citizen Portal or upload photographic/documentary evidence.
Step 2: Provide the exact street name, nearest landmark, and ward jurisdiction.
Step 3: The system assigns the complaint to the Sanitary Inspector of the concerned Prabhag / Ward.
Step 4: Real-time status can be tracked from 'Submitted' to 'Assigned', 'In Progress', and 'Resolved'.

4. STANDARD RESOLUTION TIMELINES (SLA)
- Dead animal removal: Within 4 hours (Urgent Health Priority).
- Overflowing community bin clearance: Within 8 to 12 hours.
- Missed door-to-door garbage truck collection: Within 24 hours.
- Clearance of chronic illegal garbage dumps: 24 to 48 hours.

5. PENALTIES FOR ILLEGAL DUMPING
Dumping mixed unsegregated waste or hazardous industrial debris in open public plots attracts a spot fine ranging from Rs. 500 to Rs. 5,000 under the Municipal Solid Waste Management By-laws.
