1. OVERVIEW AND SCOPE
This document outlines standard operational procedures for citizen complaints regarding municipal potable water distribution, pipeline ruptures, water contamination, low water pressure, and unscheduled supply disruptions within city limits.

2. COMMON WATER SUPPLY GRIEVANCES
- Complete stoppage of piped water supply in residential or commercial zones.
- Low water pressure preventing overhead tank filling.
- Pipeline leakage or main line bursts causing potable water loss on roads.
- Water quality issues including turbidity, yellow/brown coloration, or foul chemical odor.
- Malfunctioning municipal water meters and irregular supply schedules.

3. HOW CITIZENS CAN REGISTER A COMPLAINT
Step 1: Note the exact location including Ward Number, landmark, street name, and building/house number.
Step 2: Submit the complaint through the Smart City Citizen Portal (manual text or by uploading an official letter in PDF/DOCX/TXT format).
Step 3: Receive an automated tracking ID formatted as CMP-YYYY-XXXXXX.
Step 4: An inspection engineer from the Water Supply Department will be assigned automatically.

4. RESOLUTION TIME LIMITS (SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS - SLA)
- Major pipeline burst or main supply interruption: 6 to 12 hours.
- Low pressure or valve blockage: 24 to 48 hours.
- Water contamination investigation: Within 12 hours (water testing laboratory sample collection).
- Defective water meter inspection: 3 to 5 working days.

5. EMERGENCY HOTLINE AND ASSISTANCE
For catastrophic pipeline bursts flooding public roads, citizens should immediately dial the Central Municipal Emergency Line at 1800-233-0001 (Toll-Free, 24/7) or contact the local Ward Water Distribution Engineer.
