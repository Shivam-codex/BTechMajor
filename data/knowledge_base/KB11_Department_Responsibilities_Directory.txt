1. DIRECTORY OF MUNICIPAL BODIES AND CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Water Supply Department:
   - Potable piped drinking water supply, reservoir balancing, pipeline leak repairs.
   - Water tanker services during emergency shortages, water meter verification.

2. Sanitation Department (Solid Waste Management):
   - Daily door-to-door domestic waste pickup, segregation monitoring, street sweeping.
   - Bulk waste collection, commercial garbage dumps clearance, dead animal disposal.

3. Roads and Infrastructure Department:
   - Road resurfacing, asphalt pothole repairs, culvert bridge maintenance.
   - Concrete footpaths, pedestrian walkways, speed breakers, road lane markings.

4. Electrical Department:
   - Public street lights, LED lamps, high-mast lighting, timer switches.
   - Municipal electric feeder maintenance and electrical pole safety.

5. Drainage Department:
   - Storm-water lines, underground sewerage networks, manhole covers, nullah de-silting.
   - Wastewater suction-jetting operations to relieve street and residential flooding.

6. Public Health and Sanitation Department:
   - Public restrooms, Sulabh Shauchalaya maintenance, anti-mosquito fogging, epidemic control.

7. Traffic Management Department:
   - Electronic traffic signals, pedestrian crossing signage, coordination on parking rules.

8. General Grievance Cell:
   - Cross-departmental coordination, citizen assistance, escalation tracking, and unclassified grievances.
