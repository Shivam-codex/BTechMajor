1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This document outlines citizen rights and procedures for filing grievances concerning damaged road surfaces, deep asphalt potholes, damaged footpaths/pavements, uneven speed breakers, and road cave-ins.

2. GRIEVANCE CATEGORIZATION
- Potholes and asphalt surface depressions exceeding 50 mm depth.
- Road cave-ins, trenches dug by utility providers left unpaved or uncovered.
- Broken pedestrian sidewalks, pedestrian walkways, and damaged concrete curb stones.
- Illegally constructed, non-standard, or unpainted speed breakers causing vehicle damage.
- Missing road center dividers or broken pedestrian guard rails.

3. HOW TO SUBMIT A ROAD REPAIR COMPLAINT
Step 1: Open the Smart City Complaint Portal and select Road/Pothole category or submit document.
Step 2: Enter specific location parameters (e.g., Road Name, Cross-junction, Direction of traffic lane, Ward).
Step 3: Specify whether the pothole poses an immediate vehicular accident or two-wheeler skid hazard.
Step 4: The system will automatically tag priority as High or Critical if hazardous keywords are present.

4. RESOLUTION TIMELINES (SLA)
- Major pothole on arterial / arterial bus route: 24 to 48 hours (Cold-mix or mastic asphalt filling).
- Potholes on internal colony roads and residential lanes: 3 to 5 working days.
- Road cave-in or trench collapse posing life risk: Barricading within 2 hours; restoration within 24 hours.
- Footpath repair and slab replacement: 7 working days.

5. ESCALATION MECHANISM
If a pothole complaint remains unaddressed beyond 72 hours on major corridors, the grievance automatically escalates to the Executive Engineer (Roads) and the Ward Deputy Commissioner.
